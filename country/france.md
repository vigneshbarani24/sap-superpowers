# Country: France

## Tax System
- **VAT (TVA):** Standard 20%, intermediate 10%, reduced 5.5%, super-reduced 2.1%
- **CII (Crédit d'Impôt Innovation)** / CIR (Crédit d'Impôt Recherche) — R&D tax credits
- **SAP config:** Tax procedure TAXFR

## E-Invoicing
- **Chorus Pro:** Mandatory B2G e-invoicing (active)
- **B2B e-invoicing mandate:** Phased rollout 2026-2027 (large → medium → small)
- **Factur-X** format (French profile of ZUGFeRD)
- **PPF (Portail Public de Facturation)** — government e-invoicing platform

## Banking & Payments
- **SEPA** SCT/SDD for euro payments
- **CFONB** legacy payment formats (being replaced by SEPA XML)
- **Payment format:** pain.001 (SEPA), CFONB 160/320 (legacy)
- **Bank statement:** camt.053, CFONB 120 (legacy)

## Date & Currency
- **Date format:** DD/MM/YYYY
- **Currency:** EUR (€)
- **Decimal notation:** 1 234,56 (space as thousands separator)
- **Fiscal year:** Calendar year

## Regulatory
- **GDPR (RGPD)** — data protection
- **FEC (Fichier des Écritures Comptables):** Mandatory audit file export
- **Anti-fraud law:** Certified invoicing software since 2018
- **Code de Commerce** — commercial law
- **DSN (Déclaration Sociale Nominative):** Payroll social declaration

## Key SAP Transactions
- FEC export (French audit file)
- Chorus Pro integration for B2G
- DSN payroll reporting
