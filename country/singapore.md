# Country: Singapore

## Tax System
- **GST:** 9% (increased from 8% in Jan 2024)
- **Corporate tax:** Flat 17% with partial exemptions for SMEs
- **Withholding tax** on payments to non-residents
- **SAP config:** Tax procedure TAXSG

## E-Invoicing
- **InvoiceNow (Peppol-based):** National e-invoicing initiative
- **IRAS** (Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore) driving adoption
- Voluntary for B2B, encouraged for B2G

## Banking & Payments
- **FAST (Fast And Secure Transfers):** Real-time domestic payments
- **PayNow:** QR-based real-time payments (linked to NRIC/UEN)
- **GIRO:** Direct debit for recurring payments
- **SWIFT** for cross-border
- **Payment format:** GIRO format, FAST/PayNow

## Date & Currency
- **Date format:** DD/MM/YYYY
- **Currency:** SGD (S$)
- **Decimal notation:** 1,234.56
- **Fiscal year:** Varies (commonly calendar year or April-March)

## Regulatory
- **PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act 2012):** Data protection
- **SFRS (Singapore Financial Reporting Standards):** IFRS-converged
- **MAS** regulations for financial services
- **ACRA** — corporate registration and compliance

## Key SAP Transactions
- GST F5/F7 return preparation
- InvoiceNow Peppol integration
- GIRO payment file generation
