# Country: China

## Tax System
- **VAT:** Standard 13%, reduced 9% (agriculture, utilities), 6% (services)
- **Golden Tax System (金税系统):** Mandatory tax control and invoice system
- **Fapiao (发票):** Official tax invoices — VAT Special Fapiao (增值税专用发票) or General Fapiao
- **CIT:** Corporate Income Tax 25% (15% for qualified high-tech enterprises)
- **SAP config:** Tax procedure TAXCN, Golden Tax integration

## E-Invoicing
- **Golden Tax IV (金税四期):** Latest generation, fully electronic
- **Fully Digitalized E-Fapiao (全电发票):** Pilot in major cities, replacing paper fapiao
- **Tax Control Equipment** required (tax control disc or UKey)
- **SAP integration:** Golden Tax Interface (J_1GCTI or partner solutions)

## Banking & Payments
- **CNAPS (China National Advanced Payment System):** Domestic interbank transfers
- **Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS):** RMB international payments
- **Bank of China / ICBC / CCB** common banking partners
- **Payment format:** CNAPS standard format
- **Bank statement:** Chinese bank-specific formats

## Date & Currency
- **Date format:** YYYY年MM月DD日 or YYYY-MM-DD
- **Currency:** CNY/RMB (¥)
- **Decimal notation:** 1,234.56
- **Fiscal year:** Calendar year

## Regulatory
- **PIPL (个人信息保护法):** Personal Information Protection Law (China's GDPR equivalent)
- **Data Security Law (数据安全法):** Data localization requirements
- **Chinese Accounting Standards (CAS):** IFRS-converged
- **SAFE** regulations for cross-border payments and foreign exchange
- **Cybersecurity Law:** Data stored in China for China operations

## Key SAP Transactions
- Golden Tax Interface integration
- Fapiao generation and verification
- SAFE reporting for foreign exchange
