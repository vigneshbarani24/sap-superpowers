# Region: European Union (EU-Wide)

## Tax System
- **VAT (common framework):** Directive 2006/112/EC, rates set by member states (min 15% standard)
- **VAT ID validation:** VIES (VAT Information Exchange System) for B2B intra-EU
- **Reverse charge mechanism:** Intra-EU B2B services and goods
- **OSS (One-Stop Shop):** Single VAT registration for cross-border B2C e-commerce
- **IOSS (Import OSS):** For low-value imports ≤ €150

## E-Invoicing
- **ViDA (VAT in the Digital Age):** EU-wide e-invoicing and digital reporting mandate (phased 2028+)
- **Peppol:** Pan-European e-invoicing network
- **EN 16931:** European e-invoicing standard
- **Country variations:** Each member state has local requirements on top of EU framework

## Intra-EU Reporting
- **Intrastat:** Statistical reporting for intra-EU goods movement (dispatch/arrival)
- **EC Sales List:** Reporting of intra-EU supplies (goods and services)
- **SAF-T (Standard Audit File for Tax):** OECD standard adopted by several EU states

## Banking & Payments
- **SEPA:** Single Euro Payments Area — SCT (credit transfer), SDD (direct debit), SCT Inst (instant)
- **TARGET2:** ECB settlement system for high-value interbank
- **ISO 20022:** Standard messaging format (pain.001, camt.053)
- **PSD2:** Payment services directive — open banking APIs

## Regulatory
- **GDPR (Regulation 2016/679):** Data protection — applies to all member states
- **IFRS (mandatory for listed companies):** International Financial Reporting Standards
- **EU Taxonomy:** Sustainability reporting classification
- **CSRD:** Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (phased from 2024)
- **CBAM:** Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (phased from 2026)
- **DAC7:** Digital platform reporting to tax authorities

## Key Cross-Border Considerations
- **Transfer pricing:** Arm's length documentation (OECD TP Guidelines)
- **Permanent establishment** risk for cross-border activities
- **Withholding tax** on interest/royalties (Parent-Subsidiary Directive exemptions)
- **Anti-tax avoidance (ATAD):** Interest limitation, CFC rules, exit taxation
