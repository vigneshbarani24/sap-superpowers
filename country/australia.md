# Country: Australia

## Tax System
- **GST:** Flat 10% (Goods and Services Tax)
- **BAS (Business Activity Statement):** Quarterly/monthly GST reporting to ATO
- **PAYG withholding:** Pay-as-you-go income tax withholding
- **FBT (Fringe Benefits Tax):** Tax on non-cash employee benefits
- **SAP config:** Tax procedure TAXAU

## E-Invoicing
- **Peppol** — adopted as national e-invoicing framework
- **ATO Peppol SMP/SML** — Peppol access points registered with ATO
- B2G e-invoicing via Peppol (encouraged, not yet mandatory for all)

## Banking & Payments
- **Direct Entry (DE):** ABA/BECS format for domestic payments
- **NPP (New Payments Platform):** Real-time payments via PayID
- **SWIFT** for international transfers
- **Payment format:** ABA (Australian Bankers' Association) file format
- **Bank statement:** MT940 or BAI2

## Date & Currency
- **Date format:** DD/MM/YYYY
- **Currency:** AUD (A$)
- **Decimal notation:** 1,234.56
- **Fiscal year:** July 1 – June 30

## Regulatory
- **Privacy Act 1988** (Australian Privacy Principles)
- **STP (Single Touch Payroll):** Real-time payroll reporting to ATO
- **AASB (Australian Accounting Standards Board):** IFRS-based
- **APRA** regulations for financial services
- **Modern Slavery Act** — supply chain transparency

## Key SAP Transactions
- BAS reporting for GST
- STP payroll integration
- ABA payment file generation
