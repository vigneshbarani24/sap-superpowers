# Industry: Construction & Engineering

## Business Characteristics
- Project-driven business with long cycles (months to years)
- Progress billing (percentage of completion, milestone-based)
- Subcontractor management and retention
- Equipment and heavy asset management
- Multi-site resource allocation

## Key SAP Modules
| Module | Construction-Specific Features |
|--------|-------------------------------|
| **PS** | Project System — WBS, networks, milestones, resource planning |
| **CO** | Project cost collection, earned value analysis, WIP settlement |
| **FI** | Progress billing, retention management, joint venture accounting |
| **MM** | Site procurement, subcontractor services, equipment rental |
| **PM** | Heavy equipment maintenance, fleet management |
| **HR** | Time management for field workers, per-diem calculations |

## Key Processes
1. **Bid → Award → Mobilization → Execution → Demobilization → Close-out**
2. **Progress billing:** WBS milestone → Measurement → Invoice → Retention release
3. **Cost management:** Budget → Commitments → Actuals → Earned Value → Forecast
4. **Subcontractor:** PO → Service entry → Verification → Payment with retention
5. **Equipment:** Allocation → Deployment → Utilization tracking → Maintenance → Return

## SAP Industry Solutions
- SAP S/4HANA for Project Services
- SAP Commercial Project Management

## Compliance & Regulatory
- OSHA / health and safety reporting
- Environmental impact assessments
- Building codes and permit tracking
- Prevailing wage / Davis-Bacon compliance (US)
