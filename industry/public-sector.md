# Industry: Public Sector

## Business Characteristics
- Budget-driven (not profit-driven) operations
- Fund accounting with appropriation management
- Grant management and compliance
- Procurement regulations (competitive bidding)
- Citizen-facing services and transparency requirements

## Key SAP Modules
| Module | Public Sector-Specific Features |
|--------|--------------------------------|
| **PSM** | Public Sector Management — funds management, budgetary ledger |
| **GM** | Grants Management — award tracking, cost reimbursement |
| **FI** | Fund accounting, appropriation structure, budget availability control |
| **MM** | Public procurement, competitive bidding, framework contracts |
| **HR** | Position management, civil service pay scales, pension |
| **Analytics** | Budget execution, grant burn rate, transparency reporting |

## Key Processes
1. **Budget formulation → Appropriation → Commitment → Expenditure → Reporting**
2. **Grant lifecycle:** Application → Award → Execution → Drawdown → Closeout → Audit
3. **Procurement:** Requisition → Competitive bid → Award → Contract → Receipt → Payment
4. **Revenue management:** Tax assessment → Billing → Collection → Enforcement

## SAP Industry Solutions
- SAP S/4HANA for Public Sector
- SAP Grants Management
- SAP Public Budget Formulation

## Compliance & Regulatory
- Government accounting standards (GASB, IPSAS)
- Federal acquisition regulations (FAR)
- OMB Uniform Guidance (2 CFR 200) for grants
- Freedom of Information / transparency laws
- Government-specific cybersecurity (FedRAMP, NIST 800-53)
