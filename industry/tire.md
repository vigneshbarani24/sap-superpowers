# Industry: Tire Manufacturing

## Business Characteristics
- Mixed manufacturing modes (process + discrete)
- Multi-stage production (mixing → building → curing → finishing)
- Serial/batch hybrid tracking (batch for compounds, serial for finished tires)
- OEM vs. replacement market dual channels
- Seasonal demand patterns (winter/summer tires)

## Key SAP Modules
| Module | Tire-Specific Features |
|--------|----------------------|
| **PP** | Multi-stage production, campaign scheduling, mold management |
| **MM** | Rubber compound management, carbon black procurement, bulk chemicals |
| **QM** | Uniformity testing, endurance testing, DOT compliance |
| **SD** | OEM scheduling agreements, replacement market distribution |
| **PM** | Mold maintenance, press/curing equipment, building drum maintenance |
| **CO** | Compound-level costing, mold amortization, OEM vs. replacement margins |

## Key Processes
1. **Compound mixing → Component preparation → Tire building → Curing → Testing → Warehousing → Shipping**
2. **Mold management:** Design → Manufacture → Deployment → Maintenance → Retirement
3. **Quality:** Uniformity measurement → Force variation → Visual inspection → DOT marking

## Compliance & Regulatory
- DOT markings and UTQG ratings (US)
- ECE R30/R54 (EU tire approval)
- EU tire labeling regulation
- REACH for rubber chemicals
- Scrap tire disposal regulations
