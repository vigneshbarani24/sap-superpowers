# Industry: Banking & Financial Services

## Business Characteristics
- Real-time transaction processing at massive scale
- Multi-currency, multi-entity operations
- Regulatory capital and liquidity requirements
- Complex financial instruments and derivatives
- Customer onboarding with KYC/AML compliance

## Key SAP Modules
| Module | Banking-Specific Features |
|--------|-------------------------|
| **FI** | Bank accounting, general ledger for financial institutions |
| **IS-Banking** | Loans management, deposits, payment transactions |
| **Treasury (TR)** | Cash management, financial risk management, derivatives |
| **GRC** | Regulatory compliance, risk management, audit management |
| **FI-CA** | Contract accounts for mass customer billing |
| **Analytics** | ALM, regulatory reporting (Basel III/IV), profitability analysis |

## Key Processes
1. **Customer onboarding → KYC → Account opening → Product assignment**
2. **Loan lifecycle:** Origination → Disbursement → Servicing → Collections → Write-off
3. **Payment processing:** Initiation → Clearing → Settlement → Reconciliation
4. **Regulatory reporting:** Data collection → Calculation → Submission (COREP, FINREP, FR Y-9C)
5. **Treasury:** Deal entry → Position management → Risk calculation → Hedge accounting

## SAP Industry Solutions
- SAP S/4HANA for Financial Services
- SAP Bank Analyzer / Banking Services
- SAP Financial Products Subledger (FPSL)

## Compliance & Regulatory
- Basel III/IV capital adequacy
- IFRS 9 (expected credit loss), IFRS 17 (insurance)
- MiFID II (markets regulation)
- GDPR / data privacy for customer PII
- AML/KYC (Anti-Money Laundering, Know Your Customer)
- SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley for internal controls)
