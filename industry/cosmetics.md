# Industry: Cosmetics & Personal Care

## Business Characteristics
- Batch manufacturing with strict traceability
- Regulatory ingredient compliance (INCI lists)
- Shelf life management with expiry date tracking
- High-frequency product launches and reformulations
- Multi-channel distribution (retail, direct, professional)

## Key SAP Modules
| Module | Cosmetics-Specific Features |
|--------|---------------------------|
| **PP** | Process manufacturing, recipe management, batch sizing |
| **MM** | Ingredient management, batch with shelf life, vendor qualification |
| **QM** | Stability testing, microbiological testing, release procedures |
| **SD** | Sample management, promotional kitting, gift-with-purchase |
| **EHS** | INCI compliance, safety data sheets, substance volume tracking |
| **CO** | Product line profitability, promotional ROI |

## Key Processes
1. **Formulation → Batch production → Filling → Packaging → QA release → Distribution**
2. **Regulatory:** Ingredient check → INCI list generation → CPNP notification → Label approval
3. **Stability:** Protocol → Time-point testing → Shelf life determination → Ongoing monitoring

## SAP Industry Solutions
- SAP S/4HANA for Consumer Products
- SAP Recipe Development
- SAP Product Compliance

## Compliance & Regulatory
- EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC 1223/2009)
- FDA OTC monographs (US)
- CPNP notification (EU Cosmetics Product Notification Portal)
- INCI nomenclature
- Animal testing bans (EU, UK, India)
- Microplastics restrictions
