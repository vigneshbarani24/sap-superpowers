# Industry: Chemical

## Business Characteristics
- Hazardous materials handling with regulatory classification
- Process manufacturing with variable yields
- Dangerous goods transport regulations
- Environmental compliance (emissions, waste, water)
- Bulk storage with tank farm management

## Key SAP Modules
| Module | Chemical-Specific Features |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **PP** | Process manufacturing, campaign planning, reactor scheduling |
| **MM** | Hazmat classification, bulk procurement, tank management |
| **EHS** | Safety Data Sheets (SDS), dangerous goods, waste management |
| **QM** | Specification management, CoA, stability testing |
| **SD** | Dangerous goods shipping, bulk delivery, customer specifications |
| **PM** | Equipment maintenance with safety compliance (PSM, ATEX) |

## Key Processes
1. **Formulation → Batch production → Quality release → Bulk storage → Packaging → Shipping**
2. **Dangerous goods:** Classification → Packaging groups → Shipping documents → Transport compliance
3. **EHS management:** SDS generation → Hazard communication → Regulatory reporting
4. **Waste management:** Generation → Classification → Disposal → Regulatory reporting (EPA, EU Waste Directive)

## SAP Industry Solutions
- SAP EHS Management
- SAP Product Compliance
- SAP Recipe Development

## Compliance & Regulatory
- REACH (EU chemical registration), TSCA (US)
- GHS/CLP classification and labeling
- ADR/IMDG/IATA dangerous goods transport
- EPA reporting (TRI, RCRA)
- Process Safety Management (PSM/OSHA)
